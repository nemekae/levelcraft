DevSecOps Engineer Career Track

Beginner → Professional · 24 Weeks · 4 Stages

This syllabus is the companion reference to the interactive tracker app. It explains the program design, the 2026 industry shifts it's built around, the full tool stack, and the certification path. Week-by-week topics, resource links, and deliverables live in the tracker itself.

Why this curriculum, why now

Three shifts define DevSecOps in 2026 and shape every stage of this program:

  1. AI-generated code has changed the threat surface. A majority of enterprise codebases now include AI-assisted code, which means more unpredictable dependencies and less human review per line shipped. Engineers need to secure code they didn't fully write, not just code they did.
  2. Supply chain security is no longer optional. SBOMs, artifact signing (Sigstore), and provenance attestation (SLSA) have moved from "advanced topic" to baseline expectation after incidents like Log4Shell, SolarWinds, and the xz-utils backdoor.
  3. Platform engineering is absorbing DevSecOps. Rather than security being a separate gate, internal developer platforms (Backstage, golden paths, policy-as-code) are baking security, compliance, and cost controls directly into the paved road developers already use.

This program is sequenced so each stage builds the prerequisite for the next: you can't secure a Kubernetes cluster (Stage 3) before you can operate Linux and Docker (Stage 1), and you can't design a golden path (Stage 4) before you've built the pipeline it wraps (Stage 2).

Program structure

  • Format: 4 theory/concept days + 1 hands-on lab day per week
  • Length: 24 weeks (~6 months at a sustainable pace; compress to ~4 months at higher intensity)
  • Each stage: ends in a capstone lab that integrates everything learned that stage
  • Tracking: use the companion web app to check off each day, mark labs complete, and log deliverable links/notes — your progress saves automatically in your browser

How to use the tracker

  1. Open the tracker app and enter your name to initialize your environment — progress saves automatically after that.
  2. Each week card shows a status ring: gray (not started), amber (in progress), green (complete).
  3. Click a week to open Days 1–4 (check off each as you study, click through to the linked resource) and the Day 5 hands-on lab (check off when done, paste your repo link or notes).
  4. Use the reset control if you want to start the tracker over — this only clears the checklist state, not your own repos or this syllabus.
The tracker stores your progress in this browser's local storage. It won't sync across devices or browsers — if you switch machines, your checklist starts fresh there, though your linked repos and notes remain wherever you saved them.

Capstone — Week 24

Ship one integrated "paved road" platform: Backstage golden-path template → CI/CD with SAST + SCA + IaC + DAST + policy gates → signed & attested container (cosign/SLSA) → GitOps deploy (ArgoCD) to a hardened Kubernetes cluster → Falco + OpenTelemetry monitoring → automated compliance evidence report.

This is the artifact you put in front of a hiring manager: one repo that demonstrates every layer of the 2026 DevSecOps stack working together.
